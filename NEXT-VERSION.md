# Next version: managers who help

Status: planned. These behaviors are not implemented in the current local release.

Managers should help keep the office running, alongside their existing requests
for attention. Every manager role can participate; the jokes should not single
out the CEO or any one person.

## Coffee freshness rounds

- Managers periodically visit the coffee area and check whether the pot is
  fresh, stale, or empty. Stale coffee is distinct from running out of coffee.
- A stale pot gets a visible freshness cue. The manager can help replace it or
  coordinate a fresh brew with an available employee.
- Use cooldowns so managers do not repeatedly inspect the same pot or all
  crowd the coffee station at once.

## Organize and support employees

- Managers notice stalled work or uneven workloads and suggest an available
  employee who can help.
- Coordination gives employees a clear destination or task, with a reason for
  the request. NPCs actually respond and move when they accept the assignment.
- Helpful coordination should leave time to finish work between requests.

## Clean up, then leave a desk note

- Managers can notice trash and crumbs at desks and shared kitchen counters.
- When a manager helps clean, the mess visibly disappears and the associated
  cleanliness or distraction effect resolves.
- After helping, the manager leaves a visible note on the responsible
  employee's desk. A small indicator stays until that employee reads it.
- The employee can interact with the desk to read and dismiss the note.
  Notes are personal desk interactions; their contents are not broadcast to
  the office chatter feed.
- Track responsibility when a mess is created. Do not accuse a random employee
  when responsibility is unknown. A shared-area reminder can be anonymous.
- Notes are gently funny and specific to the cleanup, with cooldowns to avoid
  filling desks with duplicate reminders.

Example desk notes:

- "I cleared the crumbs. Your keyboard has declined the remaining snacks."
- "Trash handled. The bin is accepting new submissions all afternoon."
- "The counter is clean again. Please let it enjoy a brief retirement."

## Later training-tool direction

Managers may eventually become approachable coaching agents with defined
skills and approved reference material. Keep that future training work separate
from these game behaviors and from the current local testing release.
